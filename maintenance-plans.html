Frequently Asked Questions

How do maintenance plans work?

After enrolling in a maintenance plan, we'll set up a recurring schedule for your detailing services (monthly or every two months). We'll contact you before each appointment to confirm the exact day and time that works for you. Payment is processed at the time of each service.

Is there a long-term contract?

No, our maintenance plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. You can cancel at any time with no penalty. We simply ask for 7 days' notice before your next scheduled service.

Why do I need a Gold or Platinum detail first?

The initial Gold or Platinum detail establishes a baseline condition for your vehicle. This allows our maintenance services to focus on preserving that condition rather than correcting issues. It ensures you get the maximum benefit from your maintenance plan from day one.

What if I need to reschedule a service?

Simply contact us at least 48 hours before your scheduled service, and we'll be happy to reschedule. Maintenance plan members receive priority scheduling when rescheduling.

Can I transfer my plan to a different vehicle?

Yes, if you purchase a new vehicle, we can transfer your maintenance plan. The new vehicle will need to meet our baseline requirements, which may require an initial Gold or Platinum detail before continuing the maintenance plan.

Contact us today to schedule your initial Gold or Platinum detail and enroll in a maintenance plan. Our team will help you select the best option for your vehicle and schedule your first service.
