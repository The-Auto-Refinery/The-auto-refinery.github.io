Your Questions, Answered

Key information about our top-tier mobile detailing services.

What services do you offer and what are your rates?

We specialize in comprehensive mobile auto detailing in Modesto, CA, and surrounding Central Valley areas. Our services are designed to provide a complete solution for your vehicle's appearance and protection. Our most popular options include:

  • Detail Packages: Our Silver and Gold bundles offer complete interior and exterior cleaning, with prices starting at $200. The Gold package includes deep cleaning services like carpet extraction and leather conditioning for a full restoration.
  • Paint Correction: We offer single-stage and two-stage paint correction to remove swirls, scratches, and oxidation, restoring your paint to a showroom, like-new condition. This service is essential for achieving a true show-car finish.
  • Ceramic Coatings: For long-term protection, our ceramic coatings provide professional lasting durability against the harsh Central Valley climate, starting at just $400. This is the ultimate way to protect your vehicle and maintain a brilliant gloss.

While we operate on a flat-rate basis for transparency, final prices can vary based on vehicle size and condition. We always provide a clear assessment before work begins.

How does your mobile detailing service work?

Our mobile detailing service is built for your convenience. We bring our fully-equipped setup directly to your home or office in Modesto, Turlock, Manteca, and beyond. Here’s the simple process:

  1. Book Your Appointment: You can book online through our Setmore page or call/text us directly. We offer same-day appointments when available.
  2. We Come to You: Professional detailing tools and supplies right in your driveway! In order to lower prices even more, we utilize the customer's electricity and water. We can perform our services anywhere with enough space to work around the vehicle.
  3. Professional Service: Owned and operated by a local college student passionate about cars and with years of experience, we perform all the selected services with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring your vehicle is treated with the utmost care. No exceptions.
  4. Final Walk-Through: Upon completion, we conduct a final inspection with you to ensure you are 100% satisfied with the results.
