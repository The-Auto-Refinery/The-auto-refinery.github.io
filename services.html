Ceramic Coating in Modesto, CA

Go beyond temporary waxes. A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's factory paint, creating a layer of hard, glossy protection. Professional application is required for optimal results.

Benefits include extreme gloss, UV ray protection to prevent fading, superior hydrophobicity (water-beading), and resistance to bird droppings and water spots. It makes your car easier to clean and keeps it looking new for years. This service requires a consultation and paint correction for proper bonding.
